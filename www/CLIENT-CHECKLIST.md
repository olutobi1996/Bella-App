# Built By Bella: what we need from you

**Brand**
- Logo (SVG or PNG with transparent background) and a square app icon version
- Confirm the final app name and the colours/fonts (we're using the ones from your design link)

**Workout plans** (for each plan)
- Name, level, length in weeks, a short description and a cover photo
- Each workout: name and length in minutes
- Each exercise: name, sets, reps, rest time, and a short demo video (MP4)

**Meals** (for each meal)
- Name, category, time to make, a photo
- Calories, protein, carbs and fat per serving
- Ingredients with amounts, and the method step by step

**Membership**
- Confirm prices (currently £24.99 monthly and £219.99 yearly) and whether there is a free trial
- Whether any content is free before the paywall

**Legal and accounts**
- Privacy policy and terms (or we can help draft them), and a support email address
- Apple Developer and Google Play accounts set up in your name
- App store description and keywords
